Genbu is the release planned after [Fafnir](/releases/fafnir). Genbu is the black tortoise wound with a snake, the guardian of the north, and one of the four beasts that also include [Byakko](/releases/byakko), the white tiger of the west.

## Status

Genbu is planned, and work on it has not started. There is no date, and what it contains is not settled. Nothing on this page is a commitment: this entry will list what the release changes once that is decided.

## What will not change

Genbu ships at new addresses as an opt-in, like every release. Earlier releases stay where they are, so anything that references them by address or by a versioned name keeps working.

Janus is the release planned after [Issitoq](/releases/issitoq). Janus is the Roman god of gates and doorways, shown with two faces that look in opposite directions and holding a key.

## Status

Janus is planned, and work on it has not started. There is no date, and what it contains is not settled. Nothing on this page is a commitment: this entry will list what the release changes once that is decided.

## What will not change

Janus ships at new addresses as an opt-in, like every release. Earlier releases stay where they are, so anything that references them by address or by a versioned name keeps working.

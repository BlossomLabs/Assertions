Cerberus is the release that follows [Byakko](/releases/byakko). It is in progress.

## Status

There is no date for Cerberus, and what it contains is not settled. Nothing on this page is a commitment: this entry will list what the release changes once that is decided, and addresses once they exist.

## What will not change

Cerberus ships at new addresses as an opt-in, like every release. Byakko and [Argos](/releases/argos) stay where they are, so anything that references them by address or by a versioned name keeps working.

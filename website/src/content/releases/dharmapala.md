Dharmapala is the release planned after [Cerberus](/releases/cerberus). The Dharmapalas are the wrathful "protectors of the law" of Buddhist tradition: fierce, three-eyed figures wreathed in flames who defend a teaching against whatever would corrupt it.

## Status

Dharmapala is planned, and work on it has not started. There is no date, and what it contains is not settled. Nothing on this page is a commitment: this entry will list what the release changes once that is decided.

## What will not change

Dharmapala ships at new addresses as an opt-in, like every release. Earlier releases stay where they are, so anything that references them by address or by a versioned name keeps working.

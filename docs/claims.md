## Evidence type and execution status

Evidence type describes the method, independently of its latest execution result. `SYMBOLIC` is a Halmos property, `DIFFERENTIAL` compares generated inputs with an independent oracle, `FUZZED` checks generated inputs without one, and `UNIT` checks examples or integration invariants. `UNBACKED` means no asserting evidence was found; `RESOLVED` preserves a claim no longer made.

`PROVED` means every property cited for the row completed this bounded baseline with nonzero successful paths, no blocked paths, no counterexample and no loop-bound warning. `INCOMPLETE` covers timeouts, unsupported paths or bounds reached; it is not a proof. `UNVERIFIED` has no executed symbolic mapping. `SUITE PASSED` means the cited concrete test suites passed, within the row's limits; it is not a proof of the full prose claim. Resolved rows are historical only. [Claim-to-property mappings](verification/abi-codec-final/claims.json) and [concrete checks](verification/abi-codec-final/checks.json) provide machine-readable provenance.

The table columns retain the primary Halmos/concrete suite evidence and execution status. Supplemental inductive source proofs have their own linked manifests, execution results and assumptions. “Partial:” narrows what that evidence covers; a passing status does not remove those bounds. Ordinary failure/error claims exclude exhaustion and the exact reserved four-byte signal (C69), including symbolic properties that explicitly exclude Halmos gas artifacts.

## Halmos bounds and assumptions

Every discovered `check_*` property is inventoried and run by `python3 scripts/verify-claims.py --output docs/verification/abi-codec-final` with `--loop 70 --default-bytes-lengths 0,1,31,32,33,64,65`. Per-property solver settings and source assumptions are retained unchanged; two solver threads and a 900-second outer watchdog limit resource use. Each result records the exact command, source hashes, versions, paths, bounds, logs and outcome. Properties that do not complete remain visible. A proof covers only feasible successful paths under its vm.assume conditions and its own type/length/index case splits, never arbitrary array sizes or gas budgets.

Halmos has no concrete EVM gas model: `gasleft()` is a fresh symbol. Failure properties explicitly exclude the spurious exhaustion signal. The finite-workload concrete EVM gas sweeps and three killed guard-removal mutations, not symbolic execution, support C69. No assertion was weakened to turn an incomplete result green. Expensive arithmetic and unsupported symbolic paths remain subject to recorded limitations.

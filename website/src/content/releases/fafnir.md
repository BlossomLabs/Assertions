Fafnir is the release planned after [Erinyes](/releases/erinyes). Fafnir is the dragon of Norse legend who lies coiled over a hoard of gold and guards it.

## Status

Fafnir is planned, and work on it has not started. There is no date, and what it contains is not settled. Nothing on this page is a commitment: this entry will list what the release changes once that is decided.

## What will not change

Fafnir ships at new addresses as an opt-in, like every release. Earlier releases stay where they are, so anything that references them by address or by a versioned name keeps working.

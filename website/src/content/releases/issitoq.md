Issitoq is the release planned after [Haetae](/releases/haetae). In Inuit tradition Issitoq is a spirit described as a giant flying eye, which watches for those who break taboos and punishes them.

## Status

Issitoq is planned, and work on it has not started. There is no date, and what it contains is not settled. Nothing on this page is a commitment: this entry will list what the release changes once that is decided.

## What will not change

Issitoq ships at new addresses as an opt-in, like every release. Earlier releases stay where they are, so anything that references them by address or by a versioned name keeps working.

Haetae is the release planned after [Genbu](/releases/genbu). The Haetae is the horned, scaled lion of Korean tradition that can tell right from wrong and turns on the guilty; statues of it stand guard at palace gates.

## Status

Haetae is planned, and work on it has not started. There is no date, and what it contains is not settled. Nothing on this page is a commitment: this entry will list what the release changes once that is decided.

## What will not change

Haetae ships at new addresses as an opt-in, like every release. Earlier releases stay where they are, so anything that references them by address or by a versioned name keeps working.
